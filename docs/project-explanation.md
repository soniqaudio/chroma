## 🎹 **SONIQAUDIO VISUALIZER – PROJECT BLUEPRINT**

---

### 🧭 **Vision Recap**

You want to create a **new way to experience piano performances visually** — not as random waves, but as _structured, emotional storytelling in color and form_.  
The goal is to make music **visible** — connecting performance data (MIDI) to a living, generative visual system.

This project:

- Lives inside your **SoniqAudio** ecosystem (separate from ToneDAW, but may share components)
    
- Embodies your design philosophy: browser-native, accessible, modular, aesthetic, musical
    
- Starts simple, evolves into a deeper creative instrument or art form
    

> “A piece’s spirit made visible.”

---

### 🧠 **Core Concept**

Unlike audio-reactive “noise visualizers,” this project uses **logical musical structure** as input —  
**MIDI** is the foundation (pitch, velocity, duration, timing, harmony).

Visuals are generated from _what was played_, not _what was heard_ — this is key.  
Each note, chord, phrase, and dynamic movement becomes a visual gesture.

---

### 🎨 **Visual Metaphors (explored ideas)**

|Concept|Description|Purpose|
|---|---|---|
|**Flow / Ribbon**|Left-to-right motion like sheet music, continuous melodic trails|Emphasizes phrasing & direction|
|**Chord Bloom**|Chords as expanding colored blooms|Highlights harmonic structure|
|**Tonal Galaxy**|Notes orbit by pitch class and key|Symbolic tonal geometry|
|**Energy River**|Velocity & rhythm drive fluid particle motion|Shows performance intensity|
|**Expressive Ribbon**|One flowing line through time|Emotional contour of melody|

Each “style” could later become a selectable theme.

## 🌙 **Project Summary: “SoniqAudio Visualizer”**

### 🎹 **Core Idea**

A browser-based **musical visualizer** that transforms **MIDI performances** (like your piano videos) into **living, expressive visuals** — a fusion of **music theory, color, and motion**.

It’s not about random sound waves — it’s about **visualizing the logic of music**:  
notes, chords, dynamics, and phrasing as flowing, emotional shapes.

> Imagine “reading sheet music as light and motion.”

---

### 🧭 **Vision**

An independent project under your **SoniqAudio** brand —  
a creative companion to ToneDAW and ComposeBuddy, focused purely on **music visualization & expression**.

### 🎨 **First Visual Concept (MVP)**

**“Piano Flow”**

- X-axis = time
    
- Y-axis = pitch
    
- Color = key or scale degree
    
- Brightness / size = velocity
    
- Notes fade like trails, chords bloom like bursts
